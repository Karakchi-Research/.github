# EdgeBench: A Lightweight Preprocessing and Streaming Ingestion Framework for Edge AI Pipelines

| Year | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 | SC26 |

**Researchers:** Vito Spatafora, Austin Downey (Advisor), and Dr. Rasha Karakchi (Advisor)

## Research overview

EdgeBench is a lightweight streaming-ingestion and preprocessing framework for edge AI pipelines. The poster evaluates portable, bounded-state processing across x86 and ARM/PYNQ platforms, with telemetry to expose runtime bottlenecks.
